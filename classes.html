Your first class

New to Ritual?

Here’s everything you need to feel at ease from the moment you arrive.

  1. Book your spot

    Choose a class and send your request through our booking form or on Instagram. We’ll confirm your place.

  2. Arrive a little early

    Give yourself ten minutes to settle in and let your coach know about any injuries or conditions.

  3. Bring the basics

    Comfortable workout clothes, trainers, a water bottle and a small towel.

  4. Move at your level

    Coaches adapt every exercise, so you can work at the pace that’s right for you.

Good to know

Questions, answered.

Is Ritual women-only?

Yes. Ritual is a women-only studio — every class, every coach and every member.

Where is the studio?

We’re in Shmeisani, Amman. Tap “Get directions” anywhere on the site to open our location in Google Maps.

How do I book a class?

Use the booking form on this website, or send us a direct message on Instagram @ritualstudios. We’ll get back to you to confirm.

Do I need to be experienced?

Not at all. Tell your coach where you’re starting from and she’ll guide you through the right variations.

Can I train during or after pregnancy?

Yes — we offer dedicated pre & post natal training. We always ask for your doctor’s clearance before you begin.
